About MISP

MISP (Malware Information Sharing Platform) is an open-source threat intelligence platform designed to improve the sharing of structured threat information among security professionals.

By submitting indicators of compromise (IOCs) here, you're helping build a community-driven threat intelligence database that benefits all Decian users. Your contributions help others identify and defend against similar threats.
